# Studio and watch party audit (November 2026)

This audit is based on working in the code and on screenshots of both pages taken during this round.
Each item says what's wrong and how to fix it.

## Fixed in this round

- **Chat actions on the watch page.** Reply, report and mute were only visible on mouse hover, and you couldn't delete messages at all. All of these actions are now always visible (dimmed until you hover or tap). You can delete your own messages for everyone, and the server checks that the message is yours. Reporting asks you to confirm. Deleted, hidden and reported messages now also disappear from screens that already show them.
- **Super chats, rebuilt.**
  - The tip tray has its own message box with a character counter, and you can attach a voice message of up to 20 seconds.
  - Viewers can pay by **card, Apple Pay or Google Pay without leaving the page**. This uses Stripe's embedded checkout and charges the streamer's own Stripe account, so the super chat goes on screen by itself. There's no "I've paid" step and the host doesn't have to confirm it.
  - Every confirmed super chat plays a **full-screen alert on the stream** with a chime mixed into the program audio. Bigger amounts get a brighter chime, a different colour and more confetti.
  - The alert also **pops up in each viewer's chat**, and a strip of recent super chats sits above the chat. Super chats in the list are coloured by amount, and the voice message has a play button.
  - Hosts can preview a voice message or play it on air, and replay an alert.
- **Blur cut-out.** Background blur now uses the newer multiclass segmenter and crops to the person, so edges are much sharper.

## What's out of place or duplicated

- **Payment handles are in two places.** PayPal, Venmo and Cash App are entered under Show → Super chats and again under Shop → Support links. Keep a single "Get paid" section and have both features read from it.
- **Four "vibe" meters on the watch page** measure the same thing: the hype meter, the mood bar ("CHILL"), the Pulse tab ("Calm 50%") and the "Hyped" pill. Replace them with one Room meter.
- **Three notification systems in the studio** compete for attention: toasts, the yellow nudge bar ("1 person on stage — switch back to Solo?") and the "Next: Invite a guest" coach. Use a single queue that shows one message at a time.
- **Two identities on the watch page.** There's a chat name session (`sess.named`) and a gather account, and they have different rules. Use the account only, and let guests pick a name in one step.
- **The scenes strip and layout cards** take about a third of the studio's height while you're live. Fold them into a single row that opens on demand.

## Too complicated

- **The studio has 8 side tabs and 30 collapsible sections.** Regroup them into 5:
  - **Chat**
  - **Stage** (Look, scenes, music)
  - **Audience** (Show, polls, Play, queue)
  - **Shop**
  - **AI**
- **The watch page has too many panels below the video:** Party, Notices & Ads, What's Next, AI Spotlight, plus the shop and arena panels and the rail meta. Use one "About this show" tab for notices, what's next and the AI recap. Keep Party. Show the shop or arena panel only when the stream type calls for it.
- **The XP and badges system** gives points ("100 XP · Just arrived") but you can't do anything with them. Either tie XP to something (priority in the play-with-me queue, emotes, wagers) or hide it.

## Needs a lot of work

- **The code is too big to change safely.** `public/app.js` is 8,349 lines in one module, `watch.js` is 2,769 lines, and the CSS has grown by appending overrides (`!important`, 15 media blocks in `watch.css`). Split it into modules (stage renderer, chat, payments, AI, go-live, editor) and move all colours to tokens. Every feature we add is slower and riskier until this is done.
- **Manual super chat confirmation** ("I've paid" through PayPal, Venmo or Cash App) is trust-based, and the host has to check each one by hand. Make card the default once Stripe is connected, and keep the app links as a fallback.
- **The look of the stage graphics** (alerts, scoreboard, bounty board, lower thirds) comes from fixed code. Make them themeable from the brand kit, with 3–4 alert styles to choose from.

## What's dumb, and how to fix it

1. **Making viewers pay in another app and then type "I've paid".** Fixed: they now pay by card on the page and it confirms itself.
2. **Sound alerts that only the host hears.** Fixed: the chime now goes into the stream mix.
3. **Hover-only chat actions on touch screens.** Fixed.
4. **Four meters showing the same mood.** Merge them (see above).
5. **The same PayPal handle entered twice.** Use one "Get paid" section.
6. **A tip button in the chat input and another in the toolbar** that used to open different things. They now open the same tray. Remove the toolbar one on small screens.

## Fix plan

1. **Week 1: bugs.** Items 1–7 in the table, plus theme screenshot tests.
2. **Week 2: simplify.**
   - Studio to 5 tabs.
   - Watch panels to Party + About.
   - One meter, one notification queue, one "Get paid" section.
   - Remove `#wSuperDlg`.
3. **Weeks 3–4: architecture.**
   - Split `app.js` and `watch.js` into modules.
   - Move CSS to tokens.
   - Server-sent events for the watch feed.
   - A Stripe webhook as a backup for the client's "paid?" check.

## Ten new ideas

1. **Hot seat.** A super chat above a threshold the host sets lets the viewer join the stage on camera for 60 seconds. This builds on the existing join and play-with-me queue.
2. **Buy without leaving.** Use the same embedded checkout for products in live shopping, with a drop timer and a live stock counter on the stream ("12 left!"). When someone buys, an alert plays: "Riley just bought the hoodie".
3. **Goals that change the show.** Community goals unlock effects on stream: at $50 the stream goes into confetti mode, at $100 the host plays the next round with chat's pick, at $250 the overlay changes.
4. **AI catch-up.** A "Missed 10 minutes?" card on the watch page, built from captions and chat, with jump-to-moment links.
5. **Auto shorts.** After the stream, gather cuts the top moments into vertical clips with captions, ready for TikTok and Reels. Top moments are hype spikes, super chats and bounty completions.
6. **Watch-party rooms.** Friends open a private side room inside the watch page with their own chat (and voice later), synced to the stream.
7. **Predictions for gamers.** Viewers stake XP on round outcomes, and the scoreboard settles them automatically. The leaderboard then feeds Top fans.
8. **Voice super chats on air in one tap.** The voice clip plays on stream with an animated waveform and the viewer's name. Music ducks automatically.
9. **Live translation.** Captions and chat are translated into each viewer's language, so one stream works for a global audience.
10. **Super chat "moments".** Each paid super chat becomes a short replay card that the viewer can share ("I got a shout-out on @host's stream"). This is free promotion for the streamer.
